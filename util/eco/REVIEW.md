# Reviewing the ECO stack

The stack claims one thing: every RTL file it delivers is the compiler's own
printer applied to a Verilog AST that was proved equivalent to ordinary NEW
by an independent checker, from captures whose every byte is hash-bound, and
that claim can be re-established at any time from roots the verifier's
caller supplies. Everything that chooses or reports (matching, candidate
families, costs, selection policy, metrics, SDK packaging, diagnostics) can
only propose; it is listed in [RULES.md](RULES.md) and needs no trust. This
file names the functions that carry the claim, the PR chain, every artifact
on disk, the proof domains and the shortest review path.

## Proof domains

`StrictFourState`: a candidate passes only when every observable root and
every obligation is structurally equal to NEW's after the normaliser, which
applies the four-state identities listed in [RULES.md](RULES.md) (typed
comparison complements, logical `&&`/`||` reassociation and reordering with
truth conversion preserved, exact concatenation-field projections, equal-arm
collapse, width-preserving rewrites); no solver runs and any residual is the
issue `four-state-residual`. Candidates marked binary-only are never tried.
`eco_proof_fuzz` checks the normaliser's verdicts against Icarus four-state
simulation (Icarus evaluates `x ? z : z` to `z`, so the equal-arm collapse is
validated against Icarus semantics).

`Binary`: residual roots and obligations become a Yosys SAT miter whose
shared inputs are the corresponding typed leaves neither side drives (ports,
state, opaque-item outputs); drivers inside a cone must be `semanticExpr`
(no X/Z literals except the printer's concrete unspecified-value policies,
no calls, strings or macros); `sat -prove mismatch 0` within `soProofSeconds`,
`unresolved` on timeout or any tool failure. This is two-state equivalence
of the settled composition with corresponding ordinary-NEW state and fixed
children; it does not establish four-state equivalence and cannot waive an
X/Z counterexample. The invariant consumer proves in this domain only.

Both domains: ports, parameters, DPI declarations, child instances and their
output connections, sequential skeletons, resets, initial blocks and effects
are identical; no reset-completion, reachability, initialisation,
delta-cycle, timing or QoR fact is assumed or established. Every receipt
carries this scope sentence.
